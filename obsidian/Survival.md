## Ideas

- [ ] [[Survival - Authored buildings and structures]]
	Named-site chunks (Exeter, Tintagel, Restormel, Bodmin, the Hurlers, …) currently only get a biome flavor — the `‡ ╫ Ω` overmap glyphs are the *only* indication you've arrived. Need real interiors: walls, doors, floors as new `TerrainKind` variants; furniture (Bed, Hearth, Altar, Loom, Anvil, Market-stall, Pew) as new `Decoration` variants. Per Cornwall-World.md §4.1: each town site is a 5×5 authored chunk region (9×9 for Exeter cathedral city, 3×3 for stone circles, 5×3 for Tintagel + Sea), so the layouts are bounded. Two-tier architecture: **hand-coded factory functions** in `src/authored/<site>.rs` for special-interactive chunks (cathedral nave, Excalibur in Dozmary, Tintagel keep), **CP437 layout files** in `assets/authored/<site>/<dx>_<dy>.txt` with a legend for the bulk of town tile-work. Suggested v1 slice: Wall/Door/StoneFloor/WoodFloor TerrainKinds + Bed/Hearth/Altar Furniture; one hand-coded site (Tintagel keep, since the overmap already points there and the player can fast-travel to it); skip the asset-file loader until a second site is needed. Hooks `chunkgen::generate_chunk` to short-circuit on authored chunks (skip noise/coverage; load from factory or layout). Doors as walkable terrain that blocks sight when "closed" (need an open/closed state, probably on Decoration). Town-edge chunks beyond the authored inner ring stay procgen with hedgerow / midden / market-garden flavor. Future cards: NPCs to inhabit the buildings; locked doors + keys; siege damage; building destruction (Wound system).
- [ ] [[Survival - Ambient effects - Brainstorm]]
	Hub card for ambient effects (lighting, fire, wind, water). Catalog of candidates + decisions. Branches into the four follow-up cards below.
- [ ] [[Survival - Ambient effects - Bayer dither lighting]]
	Per-pixel 4×4 Bayer dither (4 levels) inside each 16×16 cell, replacing the smooth FOV/light blend at `main.rs:1019-1046`. Same dirty-cell cost as today.
- [ ] [[Survival - Ambient effects - Glyph cycle framework and demo]]
	`GlyphCycle` effect type + a standalone `cargo --example glyph_cycle_explorer` binary for A/B-ing fire-glyph cycle candidates on desktop and Miyoo.
- [ ] [[Survival - Ambient effects - Weather state and wind vector]]
	`WeatherState { Calm/Breezy/Gusty/Storm, Dir8 wind_dir, u8 intensity }` state machine. Save-persistent (schema bump + migration). Consumers: smoke drift, future fire-spread, future leaves/rain.
- [ ] [[Survival - Ambient effects - Smoke particles and FOV]]
	Transient `SmokeField` particle list emitted from lit fires; drifts on the weather wind vector. Each smoke cell adds a fixed single-cell visibility penalty in `recompute_fov`. Not saved.

- [ ] [[Survival - Combat - Damage math and hit roll]]

Bash/cut/stab damage triplet, weapon-vs-armor rock-paper-scissors. Per-body-part HP (torso ~80, head ~40, limbs ~60). Binary crippling at 0 HP (drop weapon / move-cost x2 / death on head or torso). Aimed shots for both melee and ranged. Supersedes the old Combat math foundation card.
